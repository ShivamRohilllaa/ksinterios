# KS Interio image sources

Downloaded on 5 October 2026. These are illustrative stock interiors used to establish the site's visual direction. They are **not photographs of completed KS Interio projects**, and must not be represented as such. The supplied website labels the imagery as design inspiration. Replace these with the business's approved project photography when available.

All assets are served locally from `dist/assets/photos/`, converted to WebP at quality 80. No external image requests are needed at runtime. All images were visually inspected after downloading.
